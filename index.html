Why is this a static website?

I felt I shouldn't overengineer my site. From my understanding through research, the purpose of a portfolio website is to:

1. Introduce yourself - Who you are, where you are located, and your experience.

2. Demonstrate what you can do and have done through your projects and experience.

3. Provide ways to contact you.

I've tried to structure my portfolio site to match these criteria to the best of my abilities. My portfolio site shouldn't need to demonstrate my skills. That role should be left to my work experience and projects. After all, there is no persistent data or complex logic required to provide the above criteria. Nor are there any state management or app like functionalities. So a backend and frontend framework aren't necessary, and adding them would be considered bloat. The portfolio being plain, static HTML and CSS allows it to be easily hosted and load incredibly quick.
